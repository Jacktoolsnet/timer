- Primary interest: colorful toy/block-style monster-truck racing and stunt animations.
- Secondary interest: block-game aesthetics and family co-viewing. This is not Roblox gameplay.
- Accessible hook: Red versus Blue, a visible loop and jump, four laps, an uncertain winner.
